AusIMM conference paper

Maximising asset value through optimal block by block decisions on marginal material

E Deucker, F Phillips and J Kato

Cut-off grades average economics across whole zones, but two blocks of the same grade can be worth very different amounts depending on what they cost to reach, when spare capacity exists, and what higher-value ore they delay. This paper moves that economic logic into the schedule, so each underground block is kept or left based on its value in context.

In the case study, block-by-block valuation lifted NPV from $288M to as much as $335M, a 17% improvement over the conventional approach.

MBA research, Torrens University

Improving strategic capital allocation frameworks for mining project investment in multi-asset companies

E Deucker

Rate-and-rank methods judge projects one at a time, which can leave a portfolio badly sequenced or over-exposed to the same risks. This research proposes a three-stage framework: screen projects against strategic pillars, use multi-objective optimisation to find financially efficient portfolios, then bring back judgement on geography, resilience and sustainability.

The result is a repeatable, auditable process that makes trade-offs such as capital intensity against cash flow and emissions explicit for executives.

13th AusIMM Underground Operators' Conference

Productivity improvement through collaborative shift planning and tracking

E Deucker and A White

The easiest short-term plan is often the opposite of the most valuable one. At Gold Fields' Hamlet mine, a web-based system built with Deswik turned the 13-week plan into a detailed weekly shift schedule that planners and shift bosses could review together and track against live production data.

It replaced a complex set of expert-maintained spreadsheets and kept critical path activities on time.
